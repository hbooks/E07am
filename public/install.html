Install manually

Follow the steps for your device if the button above didn't work

1

Open in Chrome

Go to app.hpbooks.uk in Chrome on your Android device.

2

Open the menu

Tap the three-dot menu in the top-right corner of Chrome.

3

Tap Install app

Select Install app or Add to Home screen. The wording varies by Chrome version.

4

Confirm

Tap Install to confirm. The app icon appears on your home screen.

1

Open in Safari

Go to app.hpbooks.uk in Safari. Chrome and other browsers on iOS do not support this.

2

Tap Share

Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen. It looks like a square with an arrow pointing up.

3

Add to Home Screen

Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.

4

Confirm

Tap Add in the top-right corner. The app icon appears on your home screen.

1

Open in Chrome or Edge

Go to app.hpbooks.uk in Chrome or Edge on your computer.

2

Look for the install icon

In the address bar you will see an icon of a small screen with a down arrow. Click it.

3

Confirm install

Click Install. The app opens in its own window with no browser chrome.
